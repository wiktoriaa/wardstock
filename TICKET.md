# WS-27: Suggested orders too low — please add a buffer

**From:** Grace Phiri, Pharmacist, Kasungu District Medical Store
**Priority:** High

---

Hi team,

Since the last release the ward managers keep telling us the suggested orders are too low.

Example: Kasungu District Hospital, maternity ward (`KSG-MAT`), Oxytocin 10 IU. They ran out for about two weeks in March. WardStock suggested **144 ampoules** for the April delivery, and the ward in-charge says they need around **170** to get through the next three months.

As you know, the AMC is the average consumption over the last 6 months, so one bad month shouldn't matter that much — but it clearly does.

Our proposal: **add a 20% safety buffer to all suggested order quantities.** 144 + 20% = 173, which is about what the ward is asking for.

We need this before the April ordering round on the 10th.

Thanks,
Grace
